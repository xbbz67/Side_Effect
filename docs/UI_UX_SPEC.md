# Table design and review plan

Direction: green felt, saffron medicine labels, pale paper, red Episode ink and navy Disorder ink. The memorable element is the oversized pharmacy-style Side Effects title and illustrated playing cards. Quiet surrounding controls keep the hand dominant. Palette: felt #193c38, deep felt #102c29, paper #f3edcf, yellow #e5c36c, red #b94b43, ink #253c4b. Georgia display and system sans body work offline. Original vector motifs are placeholders in a replaceable manifest; publisher reference images are not shipped.

Lobby is a tabletop invitation with a fan of physical cards, seat count and start action. Game: opponents in an algorithmic row/arc above the central draw/discard, own Psyche in the middle-lower area and hand fan at bottom. 1920×1080 logical world, uniform min scale and letterboxes. All HUD anchors derive from that world. Opponent mini-cards show public Disorders and treated markers; hidden hands only show counts.

Select a hand card, then highlighted legal targets. Target hover/keyboard focus shows expected result. Click or drag onto a target; explicit keyboard target buttons provide alternatives. ESC or empty table cancels. Preview with delayed hover, right-click or I. Unplayable selection explains why. Draw two is a visible begin-turn action. End-turn reports required discards; discard and gifts are explicit actions. Hotseat hides the next person's hand until a handoff reveal.

Dialogs trap focus and restore it. All controls have names and focus rings. Reduced motion is available in settings and follows the OS by default. Status and history use words, not color alone. Enlarged previews are clamped inside the logical world. Desktop only; no mobile breakpoint implementation.

Review at basic-renderer, interaction and polish milestones: hierarchy, turn clarity, targets, card readability, misclicks, feedback latency, keyboard access and all five required resolutions. Record actual evidence in docs/VALIDATION.md. Frame-rate measurements are environment observations, not a guarantee across hardware.
