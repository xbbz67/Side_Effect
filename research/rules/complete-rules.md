# Physical base-game rules

**Scope:** four categories in the current publisher booklet. Historical bonus cards and expansions are not part of this baseline. All rule IDs below resolve in [the evidence ledger](../sources.md).

## Components and objective

**VERIFIED RULE — R01–R04:** 2–8 players; publisher duration 10–30 minutes; current box contains 89 cards and one rulebook; categories are Disorder, Drug, Episode, and Therapy. The aim is to treat every Disorder currently in your Psyche. A treated Disorder can remain on the table; clearing every physical card is not required. [Publisher](https://pillboxgames.com/products/side-effects), [booklet setup](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/2.png?v=1778020923&width=900), [win/turn page](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/3.png?v=1778020934&width=900).

Publisher age recommendation is 14+, while BGG reports 13+ for its older listing. BGG's indexed community recommendation is best at 5–6; treat this as community preference rather than a rules restriction. Digital layouts must support all 2–8 seats. Exact retail copy counts are inferred, not publisher-verified; see [taxonomy](../cards/card-taxonomy.md).

## Setup

Use [setup.md](setup.md). R05–R10 establish separate Disorder dealing, unique Disorder names per Psyche, hidden initial hand, face-up discard, clockwise play, and the nightmare start-player convention.

## Turn

**VERIFIED RULE — R11–R14:** draw two; play zero, one, or two cards; finish with at most six cards in hand; end early if desired. Hand discards do not consume the two plays. Shuffle the discard pile to replenish an empty draw pile. [Turn page](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/3.png?v=1778020934&width=900), [resolution page](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/7.png?v=1778021009&width=900).

There is no mandatory card play. Not having a playable card does not cause elimination. The booklet does not explicitly specify arbitrary discards outside your turn; do not infer that permission from the no-action-cost sentence.

**VERIFIED RULE — R21, R22:** Tremors resists Therapy; Anorexia has no matching Drug. Episode effects are canonical in [cards.md](../cards/cards.md). A newly received Disorder is not immediately an Episode: its punishment needs a separate Episode play. This is **INFERRED BEHAVIOR**, supported by the separation of R17 and R23.

**VERIFIED RULE — R25:** no card can be put into play outside its owner's turn in this retail baseline. Victim choices required by an Episode are resolution choices, not card plays or reaction counters. The expansion Folie à Deux has an explicit exception, so the prohibition must remain scoped to this baseline. [Bargaining](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/8.png?v=1778021129&width=900).

## Bargaining

**VERIFIED RULE — R26:** players may give and receive hand cards outside their turn. A deal never grants extra card plays or permission to play out of turn. Treatment of somebody else's Disorder with your Drug, and duplicate Disorders in a Psyche, are explicitly described as house rules rather than standard permissions. [Booklet page 8](https://pillboxgames.com/cdn/shop/files/8.png?v=1778021129&width=900).

**UNKNOWN — NEEDS VERIFICATION:** gifts during a pending Episode, limit enforcement after an off-turn gift, and binding simultaneous trade timing. Digital transfers therefore need a stated room policy; preserving bargaining matters to game feel and strategy.

## Ending

**VERIFIED RULE — R27:** the first player whose Psyche is fully treated wins. The booklet permits continuing until one player is left, but does not explain a complete continuation protocol. The proposed initial online mode ends at the first winner. See [win-condition.md](win-condition.md). No health points, medical severity, automatic death, victory points, or ordinary loss-by-Disorder-count system appears in the inspected baseline.

## Rules you must not add

No automatic side-effect roll; no drawing instead of playing; no extra action from a hand discard; no Therapy on Tremors; no Drug for Anorexia; no duplicates hidden under treatments; no base-game reaction stack; no compulsory empty Psyche win; no borrowing Hex Effects mechanics; no expansion cards silently added to the retail deck.
