# Chronological gameplay observations

## Method and limits

Actual YouTube storyboard images were downloaded and inspected for both sources, in chronological order. V01: 99 sampled frames, 160×90 each, approximately every ten seconds, 16:18 duration. V02: 147 frames, 320×180 each, approximately every ten seconds, 24:14 metadata duration (listing says 24:15). These are **sampled visual observations, not a continuous watch or audio transcript**. Browser tools had zero surfaces; web playback fetch failed; caption endpoints returned empty bodies. Exact actions between frames, speech, timing of responses, emotional reactions, and precise winning declarations are UNKNOWN — NEEDS VERIFICATION.

Timestamp formula: `sampleIndex × 10 seconds`, approximate. Within a sheet: row-major order, starting row/column 1. V01 sheets contain 25 frames (5×5); V02 contain 9 (3×3). Black trailing cells are not observations. Source metadata and sheet indexes are saved in [evidence](../evidence/README.md).

## Design findings — inference, not behavioral proof

Repeated hand fanning, card overlap, pointing, pile movement, and booklet consultation support readable hover inspection, persistent Disorder names under Drugs, directed target paths, visible pile destinations, and in-context rules. Sampling does not establish hesitation causes or player emotions. No claimed reaction timing is fabricated.

## Required completion pass

Continuously review both videos with audio. Mark exact attack/response windows, trades, each dealt/drawn/discarded count, exact printed card identity, nonstandard house rules, complaints, repeated Episode handling, final treatment, and winner declaration. For every ambiguous observation retain a timestamp range and a still. Compare 2017 prototype behavior to the current booklet. Do not upgrade the rows above into VERIFIED RULE merely because they resemble the rules.
