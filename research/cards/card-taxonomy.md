## Copy inventory

Historical counts below are **INFERRED BEHAVIOR / SECONDARY REPORT**, reproduced as data from the [BGG community inventory](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/230765/side-effects) and independently reported in the [2023 inventory discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/boardgames/comments/121cwxn/side_effects_contents/). Retail counts are inferred by removing the six historical bonus cards. The 89 total matches, but arithmetic alone does not prove all individual counts.

There are eight Disorder definitions, seven Drug definitions, two ordinary action definitions, and two historical bonus definitions: 17 retail definitions / 19 recorded definitions. Copies are not distinct card types. Anorexia and Madness are reported at four copies each; other Disorders five. Tremors Drug is reported at six copies; other Drugs five.

BGG notes a 100-card box label on an older printing and removal of five cards. A publisher forum comment mentions 96. These older totals are unresolved edition evidence, not instructions to create missing cards. Booster Shot and Legal Vice are expansions; Hex Effects is a different game.

## Taxonomy

| Category | Purpose | Timing/target | Persistence and destination | Response/edge cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Disorder | Public condition; hand copy supplies attack | Own turn; opponent vulnerable through a Drug and lacking that Disorder | Persistent face-up Psyche card; Therapy discards it | No duplicates even treated; no spontaneous Episode |
| Drug | Treat one matching condition; expose vulnerabilities | Own turn; own matching Disorder | Attached above Disorder until removed | Side effects are legal attack labels, not automatic conditions; multiple stacking unverified |
| Therapy | Remove an untreated condition | Own turn; own untreated non-Tremors | Instant; both cards discarded | Anorexia needs it; removed Disorder may return through future vulnerability |
| Episode | Activate one printed punishment | Own turn; opponent's untreated Disorder | Instant, but some resulting restrictions persist; Episode discarded | Victim must comply; theft may require choice/randomness; treatment shields target |
| Historical special | Exchange Disorder or remove Drug | Edition-specific; not enabled in baseline | Exact historical destinations/timing unverified | Do not import updated expansion wording as historical card text |
