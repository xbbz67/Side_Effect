# SIDE EFFECTS — DEEP RESEARCH FOR ONLINE GAME IMPLEMENTATION

You are a senior board-game researcher, game-system designer, multiplayer engineer, UI/UX designer, and game animation designer.

Your task is to perform a COMPLETE research and reverse-engineering study of the board game:

**Side Effects — by Pillbox Games**

DO NOT START IMPLEMENTING THE GAME YET.

Your first job is to understand the original game extremely thoroughly.

# 11. ANIMATION RESEARCH

Create:

`research/animation-spec.md`

Animations must make GAME STATE easier to understand.

Avoid animations that exist only for decoration.

Research and specify animations for:

### Draw card

Deck top card lifts.

Card travels toward player's hand.

Card rotates toward the owner.

Other players only see card back.

Duration approximately:

350–600 ms.

### Play card

Selected card rises.

Other cards create space.

Card travels to center table.

Card enlarges briefly.

Effect resolves.

Then moves to destination/discard.

### Drug treatment

Drug moves from hand.

Target Disorder highlights.

Drug connects/attaches visually.

Disorder changes state to treated.

Suggested feedback:

- soft glow
- treatment icon
- connection line
- state transition

### New Disorder

Card travels toward victim.

Victim's Psyche creates a slot.

Card flips face-up.

Impact pulse.

### Episode

Should feel dangerous.

Possible sequence:

1. Episode card hits center.
2. Screen/table darkens slightly.
3. Target Disorder highlights.
4. Episode effect text appears.
5. Effect resolves.
6. Disorder pulses/shakes.
7. game continues.

### Therapy

Visually distinct from Drug.

Potential calmer animation language.

### Attack

Show directionality clearly.

Attacker → target.

### Win

Do not instantly replace the table.

Show final treatment.

Pause.

Detect victory.

Then celebration/result overlay.
